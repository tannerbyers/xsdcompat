# Code of Conduct

`xsdcompat` is intended to be a professional, technically focused open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss technical disagreements respectfully and with evidence;
- assume good faith while still reviewing correctness and security rigorously;
- keep feedback focused on code, design, documentation, and observable behavior;
- avoid sharing another person's private or confidential information;
- respect licensing, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- make project spaces usable by people regardless of background or identity.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning abuse;
- sexualized harassment or unwanted sexual attention;
- doxxing or publishing private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or sustained bad-faith participation;
- knowingly submitting material the contributor does not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove project interactions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its participants.

Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately with the repository maintainer rather than escalated through public argument. If GitHub private reporting/contact mechanisms are available, prefer those.

Enforcement decisions should be based on observable project-space behavior, not technical disagreement or criticism made in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official collaboration channels.

This lightweight policy may be replaced by a more formal community code of conduct if the project grows enough to require a broader moderation structure.
